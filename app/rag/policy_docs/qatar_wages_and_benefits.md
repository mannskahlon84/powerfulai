# Qatar Labour Law (Law No. 14 of 2004) — Wage Protection System (WPS), Repatriation & Safety

## 1. Wage Protection System (WPS) & Payment Terms
- All salaries and wages must be paid in Qatari Riyals (QAR) through an approved financial institution under the **Wage Protection System (WPS)** overseen by the Ministry of Labour and Qatar Central Bank.
- Monthly salaried employees must be paid at least once per month, no later than the 7th day of the following month.

## 2. Basic Wage vs. Allowances
- An employee's total remuneration typically consists of the **Basic Wage** plus allowances (Housing, Transportation, Living).
- Statutory benefits such as Annual Leave pay, Overtime calculations, and End-of-Service Gratuity (EOSB) are calculated exclusively on the **Basic Wage**.

## 3. Repatriation Rights (Article 57)
- Upon termination or expiration of the employment contract, the employer is legally obligated to bear the cost of the employee's **return air ticket to their home country** or agreed recruitment location.
- If the employee joins another employer in Qatar, the obligation to provide a repatriation ticket transfers to the new employer.

## 4. Medical Care & Workplace Safety
- Employers must provide primary medical care for all employees in accordance with Qatar health regulations, including mandatory health insurance coverage.
- Employers must ensure safe working conditions and comply with summer working hours restrictions (prohibiting outdoor work during peak midday heat hours from mid-June to end of August).
