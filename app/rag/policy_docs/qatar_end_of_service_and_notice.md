# Qatar Labour Law (Law No. 14 of 2004) — Notice Periods, Probation & End of Service Gratuity (EOSB)

## 1. Probationary Period (Article 39)
- The probationary period must be specified in the employment contract and cannot exceed **6 months**.
- An employer may terminate the contract during probation if the employee is found incompetent, provided at least one month's written notice is given.

## 2. Statutory Notice Periods (Article 49 & Recent Amendments)
Notice periods apply to both resignation by the employee and termination by the employer for contracts of indefinite duration:
- **Service of 2 Years or Less:** Minimum **1 month** written notice.
- **Service of More than 2 Years:** Minimum **2 months** written notice.

### Compensation in Lieu of Notice
- If either party terminates the employment contract without observing the statutory notice period, the terminating party must pay the other party compensation equivalent to the employee's **basic wage** for the notice period or the remaining portion thereof.

## 3. End of Service Gratuity / EOSB (Article 54)
Employees who have completed **one continuous year or more** of service are entitled to an End-of-Service Gratuity upon termination of employment:
- **Minimum Calculation Rate:** Calculated at a minimum of **3 weeks (21 days) of basic wage for each year of service**.
- **Pro-Rata Calculation:** Entitled to gratuity for fractions of a year worked after the first full year.
- **Base Salary:** Calculated based on the employee's last drawn basic wage (excluding allowances, bonuses, and overtime).
- **Payment Deadline:** All final settlement amounts including EOSB and pending wages must be paid within 7 days of contract termination.

## 4. Summary Dismissal Without Gratuity or Notice (Article 61)
An employer may terminate employment without notice and without payment of end-of-service gratuity only under specific severe misconduct circumstances defined by law, including:
- Assuming a false identity or submitting forged documents.
- Committing a fault causing gross financial loss to the employer (reported within 24 hours).
- Violating written workplace safety instructions.
- Disclosure of proprietary company trade secrets.
