# Qatar Labour Law (Law No. 14 of 2004) — Working Hours, Overtime & Rest Days

## 1. Standard Working Hours (Article 73)
- **Maximum Ordinary Working Hours:** **48 hours per week** (8 hours per day over a 6-day workweek, or standard equivalent for 5-day workweeks).
- **Commute Exclusion:** Time spent commuting between the employee's residence and the workplace is not counted toward working hours.
- **Rest Breaks:** Employees must be granted a rest interval of at least one hour after 6 consecutive hours of work for prayer, meals, and rest.

## 2. Ramadan Reduced Working Hours (Article 73)
- During the Holy Month of Ramadan, maximum ordinary working hours are reduced to **36 hours per week** (6 hours per day).
- This reduction applies to all employees regardless of religion or fasting status.

## 3. Overtime Pay Regulations (Article 74)
- Any work performed beyond ordinary daily hours is classified as overtime.
- **Regular Overtime Rate:** Paid at the ordinary basic hourly rate plus a minimum supplement of **25%** (**125%** total).
- **Night Overtime Rate (9:00 PM to 3:00 AM):** Paid at the ordinary basic hourly rate plus a minimum supplement of **50%** (**150%** total), except for shift workers whose regular shifts fall during night hours.
- **Maximum Daily Overtime:** Total working hours including overtime should not exceed 10 hours per day unless necessary to prevent serious loss or accident.

## 4. Weekly Rest Days & Holidays (Article 75)
- Employees are entitled to at least one paid rest day per week, usually Friday.
- If an employee is required to work on their weekly rest day, they must be compensated with an alternate rest day and paid at **150%** of their basic wage.
- Employees are entitled to official paid holidays declared by the State of Qatar (e.g., Eid Al-Fitr, Eid Al-Adha, Qatar National Day, National Sports Day).
