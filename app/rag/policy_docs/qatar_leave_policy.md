# Qatar Labour Law (Law No. 14 of 2004) — Leave Policy & Entitlements

## 1. Annual Leave (Article 79)
Employees who have completed one continuous year of service with the employer are entitled to paid annual leave as follows:
- **Less than 5 years of service:** Minimum of **3 weeks (21 days)** paid annual leave per year.
- **5 years or more of service:** Minimum of **4 weeks (28 days)** paid annual leave per year.

### Calculation and Carry-Over
- Annual leave is payable at the employee's **basic wage** plus any applicable housing/living allowances.
- With the employer's agreement, an employee may postpone up to half of their annual leave entitlement to the following year.
- Upon termination or resignation, the employee is entitled to cash compensation in lieu of any accrued and unused annual leave days.

## 2. Sick Leave (Article 82)
Employees are entitled to sick leave with pay after completing the probationary period (up to 6 months):
- **First Two Weeks:** Entitled to **100% full basic wage**.
- **Next Four Weeks:** Entitled to **50% half basic wage**.
- **Beyond Six Weeks:** Any additional sick leave is taken **unpaid** until resumption of work or resignation/termination.

### Medical Certification Requirement
- Sick leave must be supported by a valid medical certificate issued by a physician approved by the competent medical authority in Qatar.
- Failure to submit a verified medical certificate may result in the absence being treated as unpaid leave.

## 3. Maternity Leave (Article 96)
Female employees who have completed at least **one whole year** of continuous service are entitled to maternity leave:
- **Total Duration:** **50 days** of paid maternity leave at full basic wage.
- **Postnatal Period:** At least **35 days** must be taken after delivery; the remaining days may be taken before delivery.
- **Extended Medical Need:** If a medical condition arising from pregnancy or childbirth prevents resumption of work, unpaid leave up to 60 consecutive or non-consecutive days may be granted with a medical certificate.

## 4. Pilgrimage (Hajj) Leave (Article 83)
- Muslim employees who have completed at least one year of continuous service are entitled to **unpaid leave up to 20 days** once during their period of employment to perform Hajj (pilgrimage).

## 5. Compassionate / Bereavement Leave
- Employees are entitled to compassionate leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member in accordance with company HR regulations and Qatar Ministry of Labour guidelines (typically 3 to 5 days).
